An offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: every trophy with its official name, description and grade, a guide in our own words with its sources cited, a roadmap to the platinum, the missable trophies flagged, and a checklist that saves progress in the browser with export and import. Open `app/index.html` in a desktop browser — it works with the network off.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's [“Build the best GTA 6 trophy guide” bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01).

## What is inside

The checklist tracks all 55 trophies and saves earned marks in your browser.
Search and filters narrow the list, and the spoiler shield hides unearned story details.
You can export your progress as JSON, import a backup, or reset the tracker.
The seven stage roadmap shows what to tackle on the way to platinum, with a separate view for missable trophies.
Each trophy guide includes its official description, steps, and named sources.
