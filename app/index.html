How to use this guide

Select a trophy to open its description, six to nine cited steps, and sources; use the filters and search to narrow the list.

Keep the Spoiler shield checked to hide unearned story names and descriptions; cited steps and missable warnings remain visible, so read with care.

Use the + and − counters to record collectible or activity progress, then mark the trophy earned when you unlock it.

Export downloads your progress as JSON; use Import to restore that file in this browser or carry it to another browser.

Follow the Platinum roadmap in stage order, check Missables before advancing, and select a roadmap trophy to open it in the tracker.
